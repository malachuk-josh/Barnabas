# Pushpay ChMS (formerly Church Community Builder) and Tithely (Tithe.ly): Competitor Profiles

Research date: November 8, 2026. All official pages were accessed on this date unless noted. Method note: Capterra, G2, Software Advice, TrustRadius and several press sites returned HTTP 403 to direct fetches. Review ratings below therefore come from search-engine snapshots of those pages, not from the live pages. Treat review counts as approximate and check them before publishing.

Naming note: the company now writes its name "Tithely" in official materials (for example, the Nov 2025 blog and the 2025 "Simply Serve" launch). The domain is still tithe.ly, and older listings use "Tithe.ly". Pushpay's help docs now say "Pushpay ChMS", described as "previously Church Community Builder".

## 1. Company background (ownership, HQ, churches served, leadership and strategy changes 2024–2026)

### Takeaway
Pushpay is a PE-owned company (BGH Capital and Sixth Street, take-private completed May 2023) founded in New Zealand. It serves about 14,000–14,500 churches and parishes, concentrated among the largest US churches. In April 2025 it appointed a new CEO, Kenny Wyatt, and it is now pushing a "Staq Transformation" and AI. Tithely is a private, PE-backed company led by co-founder and CEO Dean Sweetman. It claims 50,000–53,000+ churches in 50+ countries and spent 2025–2026 merging its acquired products (Breeze, Elvanto) into one "Tithely 3.0" platform.

### Inferences
- Pushpay's changes since 2023 (new CEO, the "Staq Transformation", the Nurture acquisition, AI features, a free grant for church plants) look like a PE-driven plan to defend the high end and modernize the old CCB codebase. The Catholic diocese deals (Detroit) look like a second growth front.
- Tithely's count of 50k+ churches almost certainly includes free giving-only accounts, so it is not comparable to Pushpay's mostly paid, contracted base of about 14.5k. A competitive analysis should not compare the two counts directly.
- Both companies have spent 2024–2026 merging acquired products. Their customers have lived through rebrands and migrations, which creates switching moments a new entrant could target.

### Gaps
- Tithely HQ city: no reliable source. The CEO is based in Park City, Utah, per Crunchbase; my background knowledge says Atlanta, but I could not confirm it.
- Tithely's investors, funding amounts and revenue are not public (PitchBook is paywalled).
- Exact USD value of the Pushpay take-private: no primary source retrieved.
- Pushpay employee count after 2021.

## 2. Product suite: every product and module, and how they fit together

### Takeaway
Pushpay sells modular products (Giving, ChMS, Apps, Insights, Nurture, Resi streaming) in three tiers (Core/Advanced/Complete), plus all-in-one bundles: ChurchStaq for Protestant churches and ParishStaq for Catholic parishes and dioceses. Through 2026 it has been unifying giving and ChMS data ("Single Staq Giving"). Tithely sells five products (Giving, Church Management, Church App, Sites, plus Messaging/Events/Services tools) priced separately or bundled as All-Access. Since August 2025 it has been moving everything onto one "Tithely 3.0" login and data model.

### Cited Findings

**Pushpay product catalog (pricing page, Nov 2026)**
- Pushpay Giving (churches and parishes): Core / Advanced / Complete, plus a "Giving + Apps" option. Core includes the MyChurch App. Advanced and Complete include a Custom Church App. — [Pushpay Pricing](https://pushpay.com/pricing/)
- ChMS (Church Management Software): Core / Advanced / Complete. Core includes the Lead App (leader/staff app). Complete includes the MyChurch App. — [Pushpay Pricing](https://pushpay.com/pricing/)
- ChurchStaq (all-in-one for churches) and ParishStaq (all-in-one for parishes): Core / Advanced / Complete. Pushpay Insights is included in Advanced and Complete. ParishStaq for Dioceses is a separate product. — [Pushpay Pricing](https://pushpay.com/pricing/)
- Other products: Nurture (pastoral care), Pushpay Insights (attendance, giving, serving and trend analytics), Church Apps (MyChurch, Lead, Custom Church App, Per-Campus App), Resi (livestreaming, on its own site), Engiven (stock and crypto giving, "no additional monthly cost"), Everygift (giving-reliability technology included in giving plans), AI Solutions, a free AI Policy Generator, and Pushpay University (training). — [Pushpay Pricing](https://pushpay.com/pricing/)
- ChurchStaq launched in Sept 2020 as a suite combining mobile giving, donor management and church management (STALE origin date). — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pushpay); [PR Newswire: ChurchStaq launch](https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/pushpay-announces-churchstaq-end-to-end-saas-solution-to-increase-connection-and-streamline-ministry-management-301125289.html)

Related syncs: Fund Sync (Dec 2025), Single Transaction Sync (Nov 2025), Recurring schedule sync (Feb 2026), Auto-Batch Immediate (June 2026). — [Pushpay Product Releases](https://pushpay.com/product/releases/)
- Nurture now has a single entry point from the Pushpay dashboard (June 2026). SSO and assigning people to Nurture Process Queues from inside the ChMS are planned. Nurture is also sold to churches that are not Pushpay customers. — [TMCnet, June 8 2026](https://www.tmcnet.com/usubmit/2026/06/08/10395664.htm) (via search snippet); [Pushpay blog](https://pushpay.com/blog/pushpay-nurture/)

Note: ChurchMemberPro appears to be a competing vendor or affiliate site.

**Tithely product catalog (pricing page, Nov 2026)**

- Standalone products: Church App, Sites (websites), and People (described as free). — [Tithely Pricing](https://get.tithe.ly/pricing)
- "Simply Everything / All-Access" bundles all five products for $119/mo. A new "Services" module (service and event planning, volunteer scheduling, worship planning, and a worship app with CCLI/SongSelect) is labeled "new" under All-Access. — [Tithely Pricing](https://get.tithe.ly/pricing)
- Products listed on the Nov 2025 blog: Giving, People, Messaging, Church Apps, Events, Sites, and the All-Access bundle. — [Tithely blog](https://get.tithe.ly/blog/breezechms-is-integrated-into-tithely-church-management-heres-what-you-need-to-know)
- Legacy product lines: Breeze (now Tithely Church Management) and Elvanto (still sold separately; Elvanto received People-area and drag-and-drop email updates in 2024). — [Tithely product updates archive](https://get.tithe.ly/blog/tithely-product-updates-archive)
- How the products fit together: Tithely 3.0 was announced Aug 21, 2025. New customers start on 3.0, and existing churches move in phases. Tithely says it "unifies Tithely Giving and Breeze Church Management into one connected system", and over 4,000 churches had migrated as of a Dec 2025 update. Churches cannot ask to move early. — [Tithely product updates archive](https://get.tithe.ly/blog/tithely-product-updates-archive); [Elvanto Help Center: Tithely 3.0 FAQ](https://help.elvanto.com/hc/en-us/articles/34325582430743-Tithely-3-0-FAQ) and [Tithely Support: 3.0 Experience FAQ](https://support.tithe.ly/hc/en-us/articles/35175761510935-3-0-Experience-FAQ) (both from search snippets; the pages returned 403)
- The Nov 2025 rebrand promised a "unified login for giving, communication, and church management". — [Tithely blog](https://get.tithe.ly/blog/breezechms-is-integrated-into-tithely-church-management-heres-what-you-need-to-know)
- Accounting: no dedicated Tithely fund-accounting product found. Giving integrates with QuickBooks Online. — [Tithely Pricing](https://get.tithe.ly/pricing); [TheLeadPastor bookkeeping roundup](https://theleadpastor.com/tools/best-small-church-bookkeeping-software)

### Inferences
- The two architectures are opposites. Pushpay sells tiered, contracted modules aimed at multi-campus operations and is now making giving the "source of truth". Tithely sells low-priced products that can be bought separately, with free giving as the way in and the $119 bundle as the upsell.
- Neither has fully unified its acquired products as of Nov 2026. Tithely is still migrating existing accounts to 3.0, and Pushpay's "Single Staq Giving" rollout is phased.
- Pushpay owns livestreaming (Resi) and now pastoral-care workflow (Nurture); Tithely has neither. Tithely includes a website builder and worship planning; Pushpay has neither natively.

### Gaps
- No public Pushpay tier-by-tier feature matrix (which ChMS features sit in Core vs Advanced vs Complete).
- Whether Tithely Services (worship planning) comes from Elvanto's rostering or is newly built: not documented.
- No confirmed date for when all existing Tithely accounts will finish moving to 3.0.

## 3. Feature coverage by module

### Takeaway
Pushpay ChMS is strongest in giving, groups, process queues/workflows, pastoral care (Nurture plus AI Profile Summaries), multi-campus operations, check-in, and analytics (Insights). It lacks a native website builder and worship planning, and I found no native fund accounting/GL. Tithely covers more modules for a small church: website, worship planning ("Services"), facility booking, check-in, messaging, app, and free giving. Each module is shallower, it has no dedicated pastoral care, and it has no native fund accounting.

### Inferences
- For a ChurchTrac-style small or mid church, Tithely All-Access is the closer like-for-like competitor: it is all-in-one and adds a website, app and worship planning. Pushpay competes on depth (giving optimization, process queues, multi-campus, analytics, pastoral care) and wins at large or multi-site churches.
- In 2026 Pushpay has been turning pastoral care into a product (Nurture, AI Profile Summaries, care needs in Groups). That points straight at the "ministry leaders" persona a new app would target, so it is a strategic threat to note.
- Tithely's read-only MCP connector is an unusual "bring your own AI" approach. Pushpay's is native AI.

### Gaps
- Pushpay facility/resource booking: not confirmed in current sources. CCB historically had resource scheduling, but I have no 2024–26 citation.
- Pushpay native fund accounting/GL: not confirmed. It appears to rely on integrations.
