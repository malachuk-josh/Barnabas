# Planning Center and Breeze ChMS (now "Tithely Church Management"): Competitor Profiles

Research date: November 8, 2026. US market focus. All prices are as published on vendor sites on Nov 8, 2026 unless noted. Pre-2024 data is flagged **[STALE]**.

Source-access note: Capterra, G2, Software Advice, GetApp and support.breezechms.com all returned HTTP 403 to direct fetches. Ratings, counts and review quotes for those sites come from search-engine excerpts of those pages, not from reading the pages directly. Short quotes may be lightly trimmed by the search tool, so check them before publishing them as verbatim.

**Headline change for the report writer:** Breeze ChMS is no longer marketed under its own name. Tithely acquired it in 2021. On **Nov 11, 2025** it was officially renamed **"Tithely Church Management"**, and breezechms.com/features now redirects to get.tithe.ly. The product is still the same single all-in-one Breeze app, and the $72/month flat price has not changed.

## 1. Company background (ownership, founding, HQ, churches served)

### Takeaway
Planning Center is a large, founder-owned, independent company. It was founded in 2006, is based in Carlsbad, CA, has 200+ staff, and claims 78,000+ churches. In 2024 it put its ownership into a foundation and declared itself "not for sale." Breeze is no longer independent: it is a Tithely product line, renamed Tithely Church Management in Nov 2025. Its "50,000+/53,000+ churches" figure counts the whole Tithely customer base, not Breeze ChMS users alone.

### Cited Findings
**Planning Center**
- Founded in 2006, after its first tool (Services) was built for a church. The founders are Jeff Berg and Aaron Stewart, who met working at a Southern California church; Jeff Spillman joined later to scale operations. — [Planning Center About](https://www.planningcenter.com/about)
- HQ is Carlsbad, CA. Most employees work remotely across the US, and the team gathers three times a year. Headcount is 200+. — [Planning Center About](https://www.planningcenter.com/about)
- Ownership is private and founder-owned. The company has never taken investor funding and is debt-free. Jeff Berg is CEO and owner. In 2024 it formed the "Ministry Centered Foundation" and became "officially—not for sale." — [Planning Center About](https://www.planningcenter.com/about)

### Inferences
- The "50K+ churches" number for Breeze covers all of Tithely (mostly giving customers). It should not be compared one-to-one with Planning Center's 78K figure. No public figure isolates Breeze ChMS customers; the last Breeze-only claim was about 10K.
- Planning Center's "not for sale" foundation structure is a differentiator against PE- and roll-up-owned rivals such as Tithely and Pushpay. It reassures churches worried about acquisitions that raise prices or sunset products.
- Breeze is now part of a giving-led platform. Product direction is likely to center on Tithely Giving, apps, and sites cross-sell (the $119 All Access bundle) rather than on standalone ChMS depth.

## 2. Product architecture: Planning Center modular suite vs Breeze single app (what each module does)

### Takeaway
Planning Center is a suite of separately priced apps: People, Services, Giving, Groups, Check-Ins, Registrations, Calendar, and Publishing. They share one People database. Around them sit two free layers: Church Center, the congregation-facing app and website, and the new staff-facing Planning Center app (April 2026). Breeze is one web app with one flat price that covers people, events, check-in, forms, giving, messaging, service planning, and now groups. Tithely Sites (websites), the custom Tithely Church App, and advanced worship tools are separate Tithely upsells.

### Cited Findings
**Planning Center modules (official descriptions on the pricing page)**
- **People** ("Membership Management"): "Centralize all of the information you have about your church members in one place." "Unlimited people, lists, workflows, and forms are included for free for all accounts." — [PC Pricing](https://www.planningcenter.com/pricing)
- **Services** ("Service Planning & Scheduling"): "Schedule volunteers, plan services, and equip your worship team with rehearsal tools." Priced by the number of people who can log in, with unlimited teams, service types, and plans. The Music Stand add-on (digital sheet music) is listed at $5/mo on the pricing summary; the page data shows $2–$10 depending on Services tier. — [PC Pricing](https://www.planningcenter.com/pricing)
- **Check-Ins** ("Child & Volunteer Check-In"): "Check people in and out of events quickly and safely with a flexible, customizable setup." Priced by check-ins on the busiest day of the week, and "all plans include unlimited stations and events." — [PC Pricing](https://www.planningcenter.com/pricing)
- **Giving** ("Donation Management"): "Accept and process donations, track giving trends, and easily issue statements." Priced by monthly donation count, and payments are processed by Stripe. — [PC Pricing](https://www.planningcenter.com/pricing)
- **Groups** ("Groups Management"): "Organize groups, create an online group directory for signups, and take attendance." Priced by unique active group members (leaders included, visitors not counted), with unlimited groups and group events. — [PC Pricing](https://www.planningcenter.com/pricing)
- **Registrations** ("Event Signups"): "Make simple, inviting online signups and accept payments for events." Priced by attendees at the largest event, with unlimited events. The FAQ notes churches often upgrade for big events (VBS, Trunk or Treat) and then drop back to free. — [PC Pricing](https://www.planningcenter.com/pricing)
- **Calendar** ("Events & Facility Management"): "Managing your events and sharing them with your congregation is always free… You only pay if you add rooms for facilities management." — [PC Pricing](https://www.planningcenter.com/pricing)
- **Publishing** ("Web & App Content Management"): "Power your presence in the Church Center mobile app and web with customizable pages and sermons." — [PC Pricing](https://www.planningcenter.com/pricing)
- **Church Center**: the free member app and web platform — [PC Pricing](https://www.planningcenter.com/pricing). "Church Center is for your congregation, not for admin work." — [PC blog, Apr 6, 2026](https://www.planningcenter.com/blog/2026/04/planning-center-app)
- **Other cross-product pieces** listed in site navigation: Home ("Dashboard & task management"), the Planning Center app ("A free app to manage your tasks"), Chat ("Talk with your groups and teams"), NFC tags, and AI ("Powerful tools you control"). — [PC Pricing page nav](https://www.planningcenter.com/pricing)
- **Planning Center app** (Apr 6, 2026): a free staff mobile app that replaces the Tasks app. It offers cross-product notifications (schedule requests, workflow updates, form submissions), a Home dashboard of widgets, and work inside Registrations, Calendar, Check-Ins, and Giving. It adds push notifications, which the older Calendar and People apps lack. The Calendar and People apps continue "for now." — [PC blog, Apr 6, 2026](https://www.planningcenter.com/blog/2026/04/planning-center-app)

**Breeze / Tithely Church Management (single app)**
- Core features included at $72/mo: "Unlimited Contacts, Free Data Export/Import, Online & Text Giving, Text Messaging, Unlimited Emails, Event Management, Member Directories, Mobile App, Check-in & Name Tags, Service Planning, Worship Tools, Attendance & Giving Reporting, Free Phone & Email Support." — [breezechms.com](https://www.breezechms.com/); [Breeze Pricing](https://www.breezechms.com/pricing)
- The homepage sections are People, Groups, Events, Service Planning, and Forms. **Events** covers a central calendar, "Assign rooms & resources – Avoid double bookings by scheduling facilities, sound equipment, or childcare needs," and syncing the calendar with "service planning, check-ins, and team scheduling." **Service planning**: "Upgrade to access a Song Library, SongSelect® integration, and the Tithely Worship App." **Forms**: public forms whose submissions "automatically associate… with member profiles." **Groups** is "powered by Tithely Church Management," and people can join "right from your Church App." — [breezechms.com](https://www.breezechms.com/)
- Tithely's product page adds: Tithely Giving with text-to-give, built-in text and email, "Automations, smart tags, and background checks," and add-ons for Tithely Sites and Tithely Apps. — [get.tithe.ly/breezechms](https://get.tithe.ly/breezechms)
- **Tithely All Access** ($119/mo) bundles "giving, apps, websites, church management, service planning, and more" at one flat rate. — [Tithely About](https://get.tithe.ly/about); [Tithely blog Nov 2025](https://get.tithe.ly/blog/breezechms-is-integrated-into-tithely-church-management-heres-what-you-need-to-know)

### Inferences
- Planning Center's architecture lets a church buy one best-of-breed piece, typically Services, and add others later. The cost is per-module bills and separate admin interfaces, which reviewers call "cumbersome" (see Section 5). The April 2026 staff app, cross-product search (Nov 2026), and unified help center (Apr 2026) look like direct responses to that fragmentation complaint.
- Breeze's model is one login, one price, and "easy." The trade-off is shallower depth in each area, especially worship planning, and an upsell path into the Tithely ecosystem for website, branded app, and advanced worship.

### Gaps
- I could not access Breeze help-center articles (403), so detailed module behavior (follow-up queues, permission granularity, check-in security labels) is unverified from primary docs.

## 3. Feature coverage across the requested areas

### Takeaway
Both products cover the core ChMS set. Planning Center is deeper in worship/service planning, volunteer scheduling, child check-in, groups (with chat), facility booking, and its free member app (Church Center). It also now leads on AI and integrations. Breeze covers the same basics more simply in one place. **Neither has built-in fund accounting.** Planning Center tells churches to export CSVs or use third-party sync to QuickBooks. Breeze says it is donor management only, recommends a separate accounting system, and offers a limited QuickBooks Desktop export. **Neither has a full website builder in the base product**: Planning Center offers Church Center pages via Publishing, and Breeze offers Tithely Sites at +$19/mo.

- Breeze's API does not appear to expose contributions or pledges for read access; only a giving "add" endpoint shows up in the docs. This limits third-party reporting compared with Planning Center's per-product public API.

### Gaps
- Custom-field depth in Planning Center (custom tabs/fields) and permission-role granularity in both products were not verified from primary docs this session.

## 4. Pricing (date-stamped Nov 8, 2026)

### Takeaway
Planning Center is free to start: People and Church Center are always free, and each module has a free tier. Each paid module then climbs the same price ladder: **$15 → $32 → $69 → $115 → $179 → $239/mo**, set by that module's usage metric. Publishing is $0/$15/$32. Card giving costs **2.15% + $0.30**, and ACH is **$0.30 flat**. Breeze is a **flat $72/mo** with unlimited contacts. Worship Tools add **$29**, and Tithely All Access is **$119/mo** for ChMS, giving, app, sites, and worship. Giving goes through Tithely at **2.9% + $0.30 for cards, 1% + $0.30 for ACH, and 3.5% + $0.30 for AMEX**, which is notably higher than Planning Center's rates.

## 6. Target church size, positioning, and who outgrows or switches away

### Takeaway
Planning Center serves everyone from church plants (free tiers, a free year) to multisite churches. It is strongest where worship and volunteer scheduling across several ministries matter. Very large, technical churches sometimes move to Rock RMS or MinistryPlatform for customization but often keep Planning Center Services. Breeze targets small-to-mid churches run by one administrator who want simplicity and a single bill. Churches leave it, or pair it with Planning Center Services, when they need deeper worship planning. Both lose churches whose treasurers want fund accounting in the same system.

### Cited Findings
- Planning Center markets use cases for children's ministry, church management systems, church planters ("Apply for 1 year free"), multicampus churches ("Reach and resource every campus"), and worship planning. — [PC Pricing page nav](https://www.planningcenter.com/pricing)
- Best fit for Planning Center: churches running worship planning across several ministries. Skip it "if the office is one part-time administrator or nobody plans services in software." — [Church Posting](https://churchposting.com/resources/software/planning-center-vs-breeze-vs-churchtrac/)
- Best fit for Breeze: churches "with one administrator who want one flat bill, at roughly 400 names or more." Skip it "under about 150 names or if the treasurer needs fund balances in the same system." — [Church Posting](https://churchposting.com/resources/software/planning-center-vs-breeze-vs-churchtrac/)
- Breeze calls itself "The World's Easiest ChMS" and says it "shouldn't require an IT degree to use." — [breezechms.com](https://www.breezechms.com/)
- Mixed stacks: some churches use Breeze for membership and giving alongside Planning Center Services for worship planning. — [search summary of comparison sources](https://churchmemberpro.com/blog/planning-center-vs-breeze/)
- Upmarket exits from Planning Center: Rock RMS suits churches of about 1,500+ weekly attendance with development resources, and Planning Center suits "nearly everyone else." Many Rock churches still subscribe to Planning Center Services separately, and Rock has a Planning Center sync plugin. — [FlockConnect, 2026](https://flockconnect.com/blog/rock-rms-vs-planning-center-2026); [LearnOfChrist Rock review](https://learnofchrist.com/resources/rock-rms) (both vendor or affiliate sources; lower reliability)
- Reviewer reasons for leaving Planning Center: the per-module cost was "priced out," and it is not customizable ("out of the box… not the option to really change or amend"). — [Capterra PC reviews](https://capterra.com/p/76708/Planning-Center/reviews/?page=5)
- Breeze cites a reviewer who switched to it from Planning Center for ease of use. — [Capterra (search excerpt)](https://www.capterra.com/p/132513/Breeze-ChMS/reviews/)
- Breeze ACH fees (1% + $0.30) and card fees (2.9% + $0.30) are higher than Planning Center's (ACH $0.30 flat; cards 2.15% + $0.30). — [get.tithe.ly/breezechms](https://get.tithe.ly/breezechms); [PC Pricing](https://www.planningcenter.com/pricing)

### Inferences
- Churches switch away from **Planning Center** because of (a) cost creep as they add modules or grow past tier thresholds, which pushes small churches toward flat-rate tools like Breeze or ChurchTrac; (b) the need for accounting, which sends them to a separate accounting system or an all-in-one with accounting; and (c) the need for custom data or reporting at large scale, which pushes them toward Rock or MinistryPlatform.
- Churches switch away from **Breeze** because of (a) worship and volunteer depth, often answered by adding Planning Center Services; (b) giving-fee economics as giving volume grows, especially ACH; (c) wanting fund accounting; and (d) possible discomfort with the Tithely rebrand, bundling, and upsell. No source quantifies post-rebrand churn.

### Gaps
- I found no quantitative churn or switching data for either vendor.
- No primary source states Breeze's typical customer size distribution.
