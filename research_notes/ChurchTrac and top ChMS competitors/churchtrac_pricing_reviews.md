# ChurchTrac: Pricing, Company Background, Target Market, and User Sentiment

Research date: 2026-11-08. Pricing figures come from churchtrac.com, fetched 2026-11-08, unless noted otherwise. Review-site statistics are from roughly Oct–Nov 2026.

**Method caveat:** Capterra, G2, GetApp, Software Advice and TheLeadPastor returned HTTP 403 to direct fetches. I did not try to get around the block. Review statistics and user quotes below therefore come from search-engine snippets and summaries of those pages, plus figures ChurchTrac itself cites. Full review context (reviewer role, church size, date) could not be checked for most quotes. Treat the quotes as representative excerpts, not verified full reviews.

## 1. Pricing: plans, price drivers, trial/free plan, add-ons, giving fees, and changes over time

### Takeaway
ChurchTrac now sells a single all-features plan priced only by the number of "names" (people records). It runs from $29/mo for 75 names to $119/mo for unlimited names, with 10% off for yearly billing, a 30-day no-card trial, and no free plan. This replaced an older Free / Standard ($7+) / Plus ($15+) tier structure. Online giving runs through Stripe with no ChurchTrac platform fee.

### Cited Findings
**Current plan (as of 2026-11-08)**
- One plan, tiered by names tracked. Monthly prices: 75 names $29, 125 names $39, 250 names $59, 500 names $79, 1,000 names $99, unlimited names $119. The headline reads "Starting at $29/month." — [ChurchTrac Pricing](https://www.churchtrac.com/pricing)
- What drives price: "Every individual you add... counts as one name." Archived names don't count toward the limit, and you move up a tier to add more names. — [ChurchTrac Pricing](https://www.churchtrac.com/pricing)
- Yearly billing saves 10% over monthly. Annual dollar amounts are not printed on the page. — [ChurchTrac Pricing](https://www.churchtrac.com/pricing)
- 30-day free trial, no credit card required. No permanently free plan is described. — [ChurchTrac Pricing](https://www.churchtrac.com/pricing); a competitor confirms "no free plan" is listed, with pricing dated Oct 26, 2026 — [FlockConnect](https://flockconnect.com/blog/churchtrac-vs-planning-center-2026)
- Church plants get custom quotes. — [ChurchTrac Pricing](https://www.churchtrac.com/pricing)
- Homepage claims: "Affordable all-included plans with no hidden fees," "Free 30-day trial, no setup fees," "No contracts, cancel anytime," and "You only pay for the number of people you track." — [ChurchTrac homepage](https://www.churchtrac.com/)
- On its own Planning Center comparison, ChurchTrac says it "caps at $119 per month for unlimited names, with every feature included." — [ChurchTrac vs Planning Center](https://www.churchtrac.com/comparisons/churchtrac-vs-planningcenter)

**Add-ons and one-time services (as of 2026-11-08)**
- Enhanced Messaging costs $7/month and includes 250 credits/month. Extra credits are billed in blocks of 250 for $7. — [ChurchTrac Pricing](https://www.churchtrac.com/pricing)
- Optional one-time services: Website & App Build from $477, and Accounting Setup from $399. — [ChurchTrac Pricing](https://www.churchtrac.com/pricing)
- Third-party services such as MinistrySafe background checks are paid directly to the provider. — [ChurchTrac Pricing](https://www.churchtrac.com/pricing)

**Online giving processing fees (Stripe)**
- ChurchTrac charges no platform fee: "ChurchTrac does not collect or charge any fees associated with online giving." The church pays Stripe's fees. — [ChurchTrac support: Online Giving Fees](https://www.churchtrac.com/support/giving/online-giving-fees)
- Standard Stripe rates: cards "2.9% + $.30 per transaction (3.5% for Amex)." ACH (US only) "$0.25 per transaction." ACH instant verification is a one-time "$1.50" per donor bank account. — [ChurchTrac support: Online Giving Fees](https://www.churchtrac.com/support/giving/online-giving-fees)
- Eligible 501(c)(3)s can get Stripe's nonprofit rate of 2.2% + $0.30 for cards and a flat $0.25 for ACH. The pricing page markets this as "Stripe's discounted nonprofit rate." — [ChurchTrac support: Online Giving Fees](https://www.churchtrac.com/support/giving/online-giving-fees); [ChurchTrac Pricing](https://www.churchtrac.com/pricing)
- A donor "cover the fee" option is off by default. When on, it adds 3% + $0.30 by default, and admins can set 2.2%, 2.5%, 2.9% or 3.0%. — [ChurchTrac support: Online Giving Fees](https://www.churchtrac.com/support/giving/online-giving-fees)
- **Conflict:** ChurchTrac marketing and blog pages lead with 2.2% + $0.30. A ChurchTrac FAQ and support article list 2.9% + $0.30 as the "negotiated fees for ministries using ChurchTrac." The church's actual rate depends on Stripe nonprofit approval. — [ChurchTrac blog: How online giving fees work](https://www.churchtrac.com/blog/how-online-giving-fees-work); [ChurchTrac Online Giving FAQs](https://churchtrac.com/support/giving/online-giving-faqs)

**Pricing history (older structure, now apparently stale)**
- A third-party review published Feb 17, 2026 (affiliate site) described a different structure:
  - **Free** ($0, churches with 100 or fewer people): people/family management, user accounts, roles, email, support.
  - **Standard** (from $7/mo): adds giving, online donations, pledges, check-in, attendance, event sign-ups, and Church Connect website/app.
  - **Plus** (from $15/mo): adds worship planning, team scheduling, accounting, built-in SMS (250 free/month) and automations.
  - Prices scaled by database size, with 10% off yearly. After the trial, accounts dropped to the free tier rather than being locked. SMS beyond the allotment cost $0.03/recipient and voice $0.06/recipient (via Twilio).
  — [ChurchMemberPro review, Feb 2026](https://churchmemberpro.com/blog/churchtrac-review/). **Flag:** this probably described pricing that had already changed or was about to change. It conflicts with the current page.
- SpotSaaS (post first published Dec 2023, updated Oct 30, 2026, pricing checked Sept 2026) says "Earlier feature-based tiers (Basic, Standard and Plus) have been replaced by this single plan." It gives no date for the change. — [SpotSaaS](https://www.spotsaas.com/blog/churchtrac-pricing-features-in-2024)
- ChurchTrac's Planning Center comparison page, marked "Last Updated April 2026," already shows the $29–$119 table. — [ChurchTrac vs Planning Center](https://www.churchtrac.com/comparisons/churchtrac-vs-planningcenter)
- A competitor (One Church Software) still lists ChurchTrac Fund Accounting as an "Add-on (+$15/mo)." That matches the old Plus-tier structure and appears outdated, since accounting is now included. — [One Church Software vs ChurchTrac](https://onechurchsoftware.com/one-church-software-vs-churchtrac/)
- Many directory listings (e.g., G2's pricing page saying to contact ChurchTrac for pricing, or listings showing Standard from $7 and a free 100-person tier) are stale. — [G2 pricing](https://www.g2.com/products/churchtrac/pricing); [search summary of directory listings incl. SaaSworthy/SoftwareSuggest/ITQlick](https://www.saasworthy.com/product/churchtrac/pricing)
- Desktop era: ChurchTrac retired its "ChurchTrac 9 Desktop edition (including all prior desktop versions)" and no longer supports it. Desktop license pricing was not found. — [ChurchTrac support: ChurchTrac 9](https://churchtrac.com/support/questions/churchtrac-9)

**Vendor price-comparison claims**
- ChurchTrac's comparison hub gives "Average Monthly Cost" as $70–$300+ for "Other Platforms" vs $29–$85 for ChurchTrac, and says it "costs roughly half the price of other similar church management systems." The $85 upper bound is inconsistent with the $119 top tier. — [ChurchTrac Comparison hub](https://www.churchtrac.com/comparison)
- Homepage claim: churches save "an average of $877 per year" by replacing multiple tools. No methodology is given. — [ChurchTrac homepage](https://www.churchtrac.com/)

### Inferences
- Annual cost at 10% off (computed, not published): 75 names about $313/yr; 250 names about $637/yr; 1,000 names about $1,069/yr; unlimited about $1,285/yr.
- The move from Free/$7/$15 to a $29 floor with no free plan is a large price increase for the smallest churches (under 75–100 people), who used to pay $0–$15. This seems to have happened in late 2025 or early 2026, by April 2026 at the latest, but I found no official announcement. It may open room for a competitor offering a genuinely free or very cheap tier for micro-churches and church plants.
- At the top end, ChurchTrac is very price-aggressive: $119/mo for unlimited names with every feature. Beating it on price for mid-size and large churches is hard, so competitors probably need to win on UX, mobile, engagement, reporting and integrations instead.
- Pricing purely by names, with every feature included and no per-module or per-user charges, is easy to explain and a core part of its "fair, transparent" positioning. A competing product should expect buyers to compare against this simplicity.
- The zero platform fee on giving is a differentiator worth matching. Churches pay only Stripe's rates.

### Gaps
- No official dated announcement of the pricing change. The exact date and the reason (e.g., grandfathering of existing Free/Standard users) are unknown.
- Upper-tier prices under the old Standard/Plus structure (the price at each name count) were not found.
- Pricing during the desktop era (one-time license) was not found.
- Whether a free or discounted tier still exists for church plants beyond "custom quotes" is unclear.

## 2. Company background

### Takeaway
ChurchTrac is a privately held, debt-free, founder-led company in Jacksonville, Florida. It was founded in 2002 by Byron Tedder, began as Windows desktop software (now retired), takes no outside investment, and claims 14,000+ churches.

### Cited Findings
- "Founded 2002." The founder is Byron (Founder & CEO), who built it because his church couldn't find a tool that covered every ministry area and was still simple. — [ChurchTrac About Us](https://churchtrac.com/about_us)
- Byron Tedder was on staff at a local church in 2002. One profile says he built a simple database at his pastor's request. — [ChurchTrac author page](https://www.churchtrac.com/authors/byron); [faith.tools](https://faith.tools/app/churchtrac) (via search summary)
- Some software directories list 2004 as the founding year. ChurchTrac's own site says 2002. — [SourceForge](https://www.sourceforge.net/software/product/ChurchTrac) (via search summary); [ChurchTrac About Us](https://churchtrac.com/about_us)
- Ownership: "Privately owned and debt free," "No outside investors," "Built for churches—not shareholders." — [ChurchTrac About Us](https://churchtrac.com/about_us)
- Headquarters: "Made with Love in Jacksonville, Florida," with an in-house, Jacksonville-based support team. — [ChurchTrac About Us](https://churchtrac.com/about_us); [ChurchTrac homepage](https://www.churchtrac.com/)
- Scale: "Trusted by 14,000+ churches" (About page) and "Trusted by over 14,000 churches just like yours" (homepage). — [ChurchTrac About Us](https://churchtrac.com/about_us); [ChurchTrac homepage](https://www.churchtrac.com/)
- Desktop origins: the desktop line ran through ChurchTrac 9, which was retired "years ago" with no future development or support. — [ChurchTrac support: ChurchTrac 9](https://churchtrac.com/support/questions/churchtrac-9). Third-party reviews also describe it as having "began as a desktop application before moving to the cloud." — [ChurchMemberPro](https://churchmemberpro.com/blog/churchtrac-review/)
- Team size is not disclosed. Directories disagree: DesignRush lists "Under 49" employees, and Techjockey lists "1-100." — [DesignRush](https://www.designrush.com/agency/profile/churchtrac-software); [Techjockey](https://www.techjockey.com/us/brand/churchtrac) (via search summary)

### Inferences
- The bootstrapped, small-team, founder-run model supports low prices and a "customer-driven roadmap" story. It probably also limits engineering capacity, which fits user complaints about a dated UI and basic reporting.
- With 20+ years in market and a desktop install base, many current customers are long-tenured small churches that moved from the desktop product. G2 reviewers mention friction in that migration (see Section 6).

### Gaps
- No Crunchbase or LinkedIn data was retrieved: no employee count, revenue, or exact founding entity.
- No acquisitions or funding found. The company's "no outside investors" statement suggests none.
- No launch date found for the cloud product (ChurchTrac Online).

## 3. Target customer and positioning

### Takeaway
ChurchTrac positions itself as the affordable, simple, all-in-one ChMS that includes fund accounting, a website and an app. It explicitly courts small and mid-size churches and switchers from Planning Center, Breeze, Realm, desktop tools and spreadsheets. Third parties place its sweet spot at US churches under about 250–500 people.

### Cited Findings
- Positioning lines: "Church software doesn't have to be hard. Or expensive." "All your church software tools in one simple, affordable app. Made for churches of every size." "Intentionally Affordable," "Partner in Ministry." — [ChurchTrac homepage](https://www.churchtrac.com/)
- About-page promises: "Built by ministry leaders," "Fair, transparent pricing," "Intentionally affordable," "Easy to learn and use," "Gold-standard support," "No contracts or surprises," "Small churches matter," "Customer-driven roadmap," "Built for churches—not shareholders." — [ChurchTrac About Us](https://churchtrac.com/about_us)
- Comparison hub: "Why Switch to ChurchTrac? More Ministry Tools for Half the Cost." Its table contrasts competitors' "Fund Accounting: Not available" and "Website & App: Multiple Subscriptions" with ChurchTrac's "Built-in (Included)," and "Ticket queues & bots" with ChurchTrac's "Personal & Proactive" support. — [ChurchTrac Comparison hub](https://www.churchtrac.com/comparison)
- Competitors ChurchTrac names on its comparison hub: Planning Center, Tithe.ly, Breeze, QuickBooks, Aplos, Realm, Subsplash, PowerChurch, Church Windows, and spreadsheets. — [ChurchTrac Comparison hub](https://www.churchtrac.com/comparison). It also targets Servant Keeper desktop users. — [ChurchTrac blog: Servant Keeper alternative](https://www.churchtrac.com/blog/servant-keeper-alternative)
- Self-described fit: "the best Planning Center alternative for small and mid-size churches," for "ministries that want one system for everything." It concedes that Planning Center fits churches "that rely on multiple integrations" or need "modular tools for a large multi-campus setup." — [ChurchTrac vs Planning Center](https://www.churchtrac.com/comparisons/churchtrac-vs-planningcenter)
- Size and denomination: built "for churches of every size and denomination." Its solution pages target church plants, small churches and mid-size churches. — [ChurchTrac homepage](https://www.churchtrac.com/)
- Third-party fit (affiliate review, Feb 2026): best for small US churches under 250 members, church plants, rural or bi-vocational churches, churches moving off spreadsheets, and budget-conscious churches. Poor fit for churches over 500 members, churches prioritizing modern design, churches outside North America, churches needing WhatsApp or mobile money, data-driven teams, and complex production needs. — [ChurchMemberPro](https://churchmemberpro.com/blog/churchtrac-review/)
- Competitor view (July 2026): small churches, new church plants, and congregations up to about 2,000 members who want one bill for membership, giving and basic accounting. — [FlockConnect](https://flockconnect.com/blog/churchtrac-vs-planning-center-2026)
- Geography: English-only interface, US cards and ACH only, and no international or mobile-money payment methods. — [ChurchMemberPro](https://churchmemberpro.com/blog/churchtrac-review/). ACH is "US only." — [ChurchTrac support: Online Giving Fees](https://www.churchtrac.com/support/giving/online-giving-fees)
- Awards shown on the homepage are all dated 2024 (**potentially stale**): Capterra Best Value and Best Ease of Use, GetApp Best Functionality & Features, Software Advice Best Customer Support and Most Recommended, and "Rated #1 out of 130+ church management products." — [ChurchTrac homepage](https://www.churchtrac.com/)
- ChurchTrac claims to be "the only church software to achieve a perfect score of 100 out of 100." The ranking source isn't clearly identified. — [ChurchTrac Comparison hub](https://www.churchtrac.com/comparison)

### Inferences
- ChurchTrac's main wedge is bundling: fund accounting, website on a custom domain, and app all included. Planning Center has no accounting and puts Church Center sites on subdomains. A competitor aimed at ministry leaders needs either an accounting answer (built in or a tight QuickBooks/Aplos integration) or a clear story for why accounting doesn't belong in the ChMS.
- No denominational focus was found. It is a generalist US product. Non-US and non-English churches are underserved by ChurchTrac.

### Gaps
- No customer-size distribution data (e.g., median church size of the 14,000).
- No breakdown by denomination or geography beyond the US-centric payment and language limits.

## 4. User reviews: ratings, counts, strengths, complaints, quotes

### Takeaway
ChurchTrac has a large, very positive review base on the Gartner Digital Markets sites (Capterra, GetApp, Software Advice): about 4.8/5 from about 860 reviews, with customer support and value scoring highest. G2 and Trustpilot have very few reviews. Praise centers on support, price/value and ease of setup. Complaints center on a dated interface, limited reporting, and a few mobile/app and permissions issues.

### Cited Findings

- The sentiment pattern (support and value high, UI and reporting low) is the classic profile of a bootstrapped, long-lived product. A modern UX, flexible reporting and dashboards, and a strong mobile experience are the clearest openings for a competitor.
- "Cost is a bit pricey" complaints may grow after the move to a $29 floor with no free plan. Inference only; no post-change review data was checked.

## 5. Customer support model

### Takeaway
Support is a central selling point: an in-house, US-based (Jacksonville) team, free for all paid subscriptions, with ticket and email support, weekly live training, free 1-on-1 workshops, a large video library and free data import. Phone access appears limited or scheduled, and dedicated onboarding is for paying customers only.

### Cited Findings
- Homepage claims: "Jacksonville, FL USA-based support team," "Free 1-on-1 workshops with our team," "Weekly live training webinars," "400+ tutorial videos and 160+ help articles," "An active community of church admins," "Free data import," "Friendly setup assistance," "Fast human support." — [ChurchTrac homepage](https://www.churchtrac.com/)
- ChurchTrac says it will import data "for free" from a spreadsheet or another church software provider, and that the onboarding team helps import existing data. — [ChurchTrac: Church Administrators](https://www.churchtrac.com/solutions/church-administrators) (via search summary)
- Free support for all paid subscriptions, with "under 1 business hour average response for new tickets." — ChurchTrac site (via search summary; exact page not confirmed) [ChurchTrac homepage](https://www.churchtrac.com:443)
- **Conflicting reports on channels**:
  - One search summary said the homepage lists "Phone, ticket, and email support," but my direct fetch of the homepage found help center, support tickets and live demos, with no phone or chat mentioned. — [ChurchTrac homepage](https://www.churchtrac.com/)
  - Capterra lists chat. — [Capterra (via search summary)](https://www.capterra.com/p/125787/ChurchTrac/reviews/)
  - B2BReviews says phone support is limited to scheduling a call. — [B2BReviews](https://www.b2breviews.com/reviews/churchtrac/)
  - A Capterra reviewer says calls can be requested. — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/125787/ChurchTrac/reviews/)
  - Competitor One Church Software claims ChurchTrac offers "Email support only" (competitor claim, contradicted by the above). — [One Church Software vs ChurchTrac](https://onechurchsoftware.com/one-church-software-vs-churchtrac/)
- A ChurchTrac reply to a Capterra reviewer says dedicated onboarding is reserved for paying churches, while trial users get self-guided resources and free support. — [Capterra review (via search summary)](https://www.capterra.com/p/125787/ChurchTrac/reviews/6017502/)
- Reviewers praise the free intro webinar and tutorials. One said the "onboarding process needs more hands on support for beginners." — [Capterra (via search summary)](https://www.capterra.com/p/125787/ChurchTrac/reviews/?page=7)
- Paid setup services exist: Website & App Build from $477, Accounting Setup from $399. — [ChurchTrac Pricing](https://www.churchtrac.com/pricing)
- A third party estimates 2–3 weeks for most churches to be fully onboarded. — [ChurchMemberPro](https://churchmemberpro.com/blog/churchtrac-review/)
- The comparison hub frames competitor support as "Ticket queues & bots" vs ChurchTrac's "Personal & Proactive." — [ChurchTrac Comparison hub](https://www.churchtrac.com/comparison)

### Inferences
- Support quality is ChurchTrac's strongest moat in reviews (4.9 GetApp support score). A competitor must at least match human, fast, free support for small-church admins, who are often volunteers or part-time staff. Free migration help is table stakes for switchers.
- Some switchers want "someone to call" (see Section 6). Easy phone access and guided onboarding could set a competitor apart, especially during trials.

### Gaps
- No published support hours, SLA, or confirmed phone number policy.
- No data on support team headcount.

## 6. Where ChurchTrac falls short, and switching patterns

### Takeaway
Consistent weaknesses are a dated UI, basic reporting (no custom report builder or dashboards), limited multi-campus and large-church depth, a thin integration ecosystem and no public API (competitor claim), a shared (not fully branded) member app, US/English-only giving and interface, and weaker worship-team scheduling than Planning Center. Churches switch in from Planning Center, Rock RMS, ACS and desktop tools on price and support. Some outgrow it around 400–500 members.

### Cited Findings
- **Scalability and large churches**:
  - A blog relays one church that "outgrew ChurchTrac around 400 members and moved to Planning Center." The same blog calls it a poor fit for churches over 500 members. — [ChurchMemberPro](https://churchmemberpro.com/blog/churchtrac-review/)
  - ChurchTrac itself concedes Planning Center suits "a large multi-campus setup." — [ChurchTrac vs Planning Center](https://www.churchtrac.com/comparisons/churchtrac-vs-planningcenter)
  - A competitor lists ChurchTrac multi-campus as "Limited." — [One Church Software vs ChurchTrac](https://onechurchsoftware.com/one-church-software-vs-churchtrac/)
- **Reporting**: "Basic reporting with no custom report builder or visual dashboards." — [ChurchMemberPro](https://churchmemberpro.com/blog/churchtrac-review/). "Basic reports, no custom builder." — [One Church Software](https://onechurchsoftware.com/one-church-software-vs-churchtrac/) (competitor). See also the user quotes in Section 4.
- **UI**: a dated interface that "may slow volunteer adoption." — [ChurchMemberPro](https://churchmemberpro.com/blog/churchtrac-review/). "the older interface slowed them down" (competitor's unattributed switcher anecdote). — [One Church Software](https://onechurchsoftware.com/one-church-software-vs-churchtrac/)
- **Integrations and API**:
  - The integration list is thinner than Planning Center's, mainly accounting tools plus basic Planning Center and Mailchimp connections. — [FlockConnect](https://flockconnect.com/blog/churchtrac-vs-planning-center-2026) (competitor)
  - "Small integration ecosystem," with compatibility for ProPresenter, CCLI SongSelect and Mailchimp to be verified. — [ChurchMemberPro](https://churchmemberpro.com/blog/churchtrac-review/)
  - "API access: Not available." — [One Church Software](https://onechurchsoftware.com/one-church-software-vs-churchtrac/) (competitor claim, unverified)
  - Reviewers cite a lack of advanced customization for integration-heavy organizations. — [TheLeadPastor / G2 via search summary](https://theleadpastor.com/tools/churchtrac-review/)
- **Mobile and member engagement**:
  - Member app is a "Shared 'Church Connect' app (not fully branded)." Switchers "wanted one that carried their own church's name and logo." — [One Church Software](https://onechurchsoftware.com/one-church-software-vs-churchtrac/) (competitor)
  - iOS Connect app has about 1K estimated monthly active users. — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/6737914083)
  - Users report app-loading and phone-usability issues (Section 4).
- **Communications**: no WhatsApp, email templates, email analytics or email scheduling. — [ChurchMemberPro](https://churchmemberpro.com/blog/churchtrac-review/) (Feb 2026; may have changed since)
- **Worship and volunteer scheduling**: "functional" for general volunteer work but not built to match Planning Center Services for worship teams. Likely to be outgrown by churches with active worship bands. — [FlockConnect](https://flockconnect.com/blog/churchtrac-vs-planning-center-2026) (competitor)
- **International**: English-only, and no M-Pesa, Paystack, Flutterwave or mobile money. US cards and ACH only. — [ChurchMemberPro](https://churchmemberpro.com/blog/churchtrac-review/)
- **Switched TO ChurchTrac from**:
  - Planning Center: ChurchTrac claims that of 35 Capterra reviewers who switched away from Planning Center (October 2026), more chose ChurchTrac than any other product (vendor claim). — [ChurchTrac vs Planning Center](https://www.churchtrac.com/comparisons/churchtrac-vs-planningcenter)
  - Rock RMS: a reviewer left because separate tools caused "data duplication and constant sync issues." — [Techjockey](https://www.techjockey.com/us/detail/churchtrac)
  - ACS Technologies: a reviewer left because ACS support was "slower and more expensive for our church size." — [Techjockey](https://www.techjockey.com/us/detail/churchtrac)
  - ChurchTrac also markets to Servant Keeper desktop users. — [ChurchTrac blog](https://www.churchtrac.com/blog/servant-keeper-alternative)
- **Switched AWAY from ChurchTrac to**:
  - Planning Center (one church at about 400 members). — [ChurchMemberPro](https://churchmemberpro.com/blog/churchtrac-review/)
  - One Church Software claims (unattributed) that ChurchTrac leavers "liked the low price at the start, then outgrew a basic setup," wanted deeper reports, a branded app, "quicker hands-on help and someone to call," and native multi-campus and API. — [One Church Software](https://onechurchsoftware.com/one-church-software-vs-churchtrac/) (competitor marketing; treat as low-reliability)

### Inferences
- The clearest gaps for a ministry-leader-focused competitor:
  1. Modern, volunteer-friendly UI and a mobile-first leader experience.
  2. Flexible reporting and dashboards for pastors and ministry leads.
  3. Engagement and communications depth: templates, scheduling, analytics, possibly WhatsApp.
  4. A branded member app.
  5. An open API and integration ecosystem.
  6. Multi-site support.
  7. Possibly international and multilingual support.
- ChurchTrac's strengths to match: an all-in-one bundle including accounting, transparent per-name pricing, no giving platform fee, free migration, and fast human support.
- The ~400–500-member "outgrow" point suggests ChurchTrac is strongest below that size. Mid-size churches growing past it, especially those with worship teams or multiple ministries led by distinct leaders, are a contestable segment.

### Gaps
- No independent quantitative churn or switching data. Switching evidence is anecdotal or vendor- and competitor-sourced.
- The "no API" claim could not be checked against ChurchTrac documentation in this pass. The separate feature researcher may cover it.
