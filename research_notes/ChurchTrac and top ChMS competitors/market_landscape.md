# ChMS Market Landscape and Top 5 Competitors to ChurchTrac (research as of Nov 8, 2026)

Scope note: These are market-level notes plus the reasoning for the shortlist. Deep product profiles are covered elsewhere. Capterra, G2, GetApp and The Lead Pastor pages returned HTTP 403 when fetched directly, so review counts below come from search-result snippets of those pages (titled "2026"). Expect small drifts in the counts. Vendor-reported numbers are labeled as such.

## 1. How big is the church management software market, and how fast is it growing?

### Takeaway
Analyst estimates disagree. Most firms that define the category narrowly put global ChMS revenue at about USD 290–420M in 2025, growing at a 6–9% CAGR. The US is the core market, with about 357,000–370,000 congregations. A Pushpay-sponsored survey reports that 86% of churches use a ChMS (2025), though its sample probably over-represents tech-forward churches.

### Cited Findings
- **Market size (2025), low and mid estimates:** Search summaries of research-reseller listings (GII Research) gave USD 292.25M for 2025 at a 6% CAGR through 2033, and another estimate of USD 398.07M for 2025. I could not confirm which publisher produced which figure. Treat these as indicative only. — [GII Research listing](https://www.giiresearch.com/report/infi2055787-global-church-management-software-market.html)
- **Technavio (April 2025 report):** incremental market growth of USD 418.5M over 2025–2029 at an 8.9% CAGR. — [Technavio](https://www.technavio.com/report/church-management-software-market-industry-analysis)
- **Outlier:** Spherical Insights values the market at USD 17.23B in 2025, about 40x the other estimates. It almost certainly uses a much broader definition (for example, all church tech or giving volume) and shouldn't be used. — [Spherical Insights](https://www.sphericalinsights.com/reports/church-management-software-market)
- **Other publishers in the space** (no figures pulled, methodology unknown): Market Research Future, Straits Research, WiseGuy Reports, Archive Market Research. — [MRFR](https://www.marketresearchfuture.com/reports/church-management-software-market-23745); [Straits](https://straitsresearch.com/report/church-management-software-market); [WiseGuy](https://www.wiseguyreports.com/reports/church-management-software-market)
- **US congregations:** about 356,739 churches and religious congregations, per the 2020 U.S. Religion Census (a secondary source citing it). The Hartford Institute for Religion Research estimates about 370,000, because some independent and immigrant congregations never appear in denominational reports. It also notes there is no official directory of all congregations. — [ReachRight citing 2020 USRC](https://reachrightstudios.com/blog/how-many-churches-in-america/); [Hartford Institute Fast Facts](https://hirr.hartfordinternational.edu/?p=40)
- **ChMS adoption (Pushpay 2025 State of Church Technology, released Apr 30, 2025, n = 1,700+ US church leaders):** 86% of churches use a ChMS, up 4% year over year. 67% use a church app, up 2%. 87% livestream services, up 6%. 52% report bigger tech budgets and 10% smaller ones. Concern about the cost of new technology fell 9%, the first decline since the report began. — [Religion Unplugged (Apr 30, 2025)](https://religionunplugged.com/news/2025/4/30/new-study-reveals-key-tech-trends-shaping-the-us-church); [Pushpay blog](https://pushpay.com/blog/introducing-the-state-of-church-technology-2025-report/?p=16642)
- **2024 report:** 30% more leaders said ChMS would be strategically important to their ministry's future. This is from a search summary of Pushpay's 2024 report. — [MarketScreener / Pushpay 2024 release](https://m.marketscreener.com/quote/stock/PUSHPAY-HOLDINGS-LIMITED-32613790/news/Pushpay-s-2024-State-of-Church-Technology-Report-Reveals-Church-Leaders-Abandon-In-Person-Only-Ser-45893541/)
- **2026 report (Pushpay + Barna, released Mar 9, 2026; n = 1,306 US church leaders surveyed late 2025; more than half from churches under 200 attendees):** lists ChMS among the most widely used tools alongside social media, online/mobile giving, livestreaming, QR codes, texting and donor management. No ChMS percentage was published in the coverage I found. — [MinistryWatch (Mar 30, 2026)](https://ministrywatch.com/the-new-church-tech-divide-is-missional-not-digital/); [GlobeNewswire release](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/03/09/3251986/0/en/Pushpay-and-Barna-Group-s-2026-State-of-Church-Technology-Report-Shows-Church-Tech-Has-Moved-Past-Adoption-Now-Alignment-Is-What-Matters.html)
- **Historical contrast (2010, flagged as old):** Lifeway Research found only 12% of Protestant churches used web-based church management software. — [Lifeway Research](https://research.lifeway.com/?p=263) (via search summary)

### Inferences
- **Bottom-up check (my estimate, not sourced):** about 300,000 US congregations × roughly $600–$1,200 a year in ChMS spend (the $50–$100/month small-church price points vendors publish) comes to about USD 180–360M for the US. That is consistent with the USD 290–420M global estimates, so the narrow-definition reports look roughly right in magnitude.
- **Adoption is likely overstated:** the 86% figure comes from a vendor's opt-in respondent base. True adoption across all ~370k congregations, many of them very small, is probably lower. A large slice of tiny churches still runs on spreadsheets, desktop legacy tools or nothing at all. That slice is the greenfield and switching opportunity.
- **Where growth comes from:** if the survey's high adoption rate is even roughly right, the 6–9% CAGR comes mainly from switching, bundling with giving and payments, and upsell (apps, AI), not from first-time adoption.

### Gaps
- No market-research methodology was accessible. All reports are paywalled, and figures came from reseller or search summaries.
- I found no independent US-only ChMS revenue estimate.
- I found no independent (non-vendor) 2024–2026 ChMS adoption survey. Lifeway, Barna-only and Church Answers data on ChMS adoption were not found.

## 3. Which products are most often listed as ChurchTrac's direct competitors or alternatives?

### Takeaway
Across ChurchTrac's own comparison pages and the Capterra, GetApp and Software Advice comparison pages, the recurring direct competitors are Planning Center, Breeze/Tithely, Realm, and legacy small-church tools (PowerChurch, Church Windows, Servant Keeper). Accounting tools (QuickBooks, Aplos) also appear. G2's "alternatives" page is dominated by general accounting software, a sign that ChurchTrac's accounting module is a key buying axis.

### Cited Findings
- **ChurchTrac's comparisons hub:** head-to-head pages against Planning Center, Tithe.ly, Breeze, QuickBooks and Aplos, and "alternative" pages for Church Windows, Realm, Aplos and QuickBooks. — [ChurchTrac comparisons](https://www.churchtrac.com/comparisons)
- **ChurchTrac vs PowerChurch** (updated April 2026): says ChurchTrac generally costs less and includes a website and church app, which PowerChurch lacks. — [ChurchTrac vs PowerChurch](https://www.churchtrac.com/comparisons/churchtrac-vs-powerchurch)
- **ChurchTrac's Servant Keeper alternative page:** targets churches leaving the "outdated desktop version" of Servant Keeper/Servant PC. — [ChurchTrac article](https://www.churchtrac.com/articles/servant-keeper-alternative)
- **ChurchTrac vs Breeze page** exists on ChurchTrac's site. — [ChurchTrac vs Breeze](https://www.churchtrac.com/comparisons/churchtrac-vs-breeze)
- **Capterra comparison pages:** Planning Center vs ChurchTrac, ChurchTrac vs Realm, and ChurchTrac vs Tithely Church Management. On the Tithely page, ChurchTrac shows a $9/month starting price against Tithely's $72 flat rate. — [Capterra PC vs ChurchTrac](https://www.capterra.com/compare/76708-125787/Planning-Center-vs-ChurchTrac); [Capterra ChurchTrac vs Realm](https://www.capterra.com/compare/125787-146265/ChurchTrac-vs-Realm); [Capterra ChurchTrac vs Tithely](https://www.capterra.com/compare/125787-132513/ChurchTrac-vs-Breeze-ChMS)

### Inferences
- **Two competitive fronts:** ChurchTrac fights (a) modern cloud all-in-ones for small and mid churches (Planning Center, Tithely/Breeze, Realm), and (b) legacy desktop and accounting tools it tries to displace (PowerChurch, Church Windows, Servant Keeper, QuickBooks, Aplos, IconCMO).
- **The large-church players are not head-to-head rivals:** Pushpay and Rock RMS rarely appear as ChurchTrac alternatives on review sites. They matter for the overall market picture but not as direct rivals in ChurchTrac's core under-2,000-member segment.

### Gaps
- The full ranked lists on the Capterra, G2 and GetApp ChurchTrac alternatives pages could not be retrieved (403). Only fragments surfaced through search.

## 4. Recommended top 5 for the comparison, segmented, with near misses

### Takeaway
Recommended top 5: **Planning Center, Tithely Church Management (formerly Breeze), Pushpay ChurchStaq (formerly CCB), Realm by ACS Technologies, and Rock RMS.** Together they cover the small-church all-in-one segment where ChurchTrac competes and the large-church platform tier that sets feature expectations. Subsplash is the closest miss and should replace Rock RMS if the comparison centers on the member mobile app.

### Cited Findings (evidence per pick; details in sections 2–3)
1. **Planning Center** (small to large, modular).
   - Largest claimed footprint (100k+ churches) and the most Capterra reviews (~1,138, 4.7).
   - Appears on both the ChurchTrac and Pushpay 2026 lists.
   - First head-to-head on ChurchTrac's comparisons hub.
   - Free tier, then about $15 per module up to about $239/month (per Pushpay's list).
   - [faith.tools](https://faith.tools/app/planning-center); [ChurchTrac comparisons](https://www.churchtrac.com/comparisons); [Pushpay blog](https://pushpay.com/blog/best-church-management-software-for-2026/)
2. **Tithely Church Management (Breeze), with Tithely Giving** (small-church simple all-in-one). The closest like-for-like competitor to ChurchTrac.
   - Tithely claims 50k+ churches.
   - Capterra 4.8 from ~623 reviews.
   - Flat $72/month "Church Management + Free Giving."
   - ChurchTrac publishes separate comparisons against both Breeze and Tithe.ly.
   - Rebranded from Breeze on Nov 11, 2025.
   - [Tithely blog](https://get.tithe.ly/blog/breezechms-is-integrated-into-tithely-church-management-heres-what-you-need-to-know); [Tithely compare](https://get.tithe.ly:443/compare); [ToolRadar](https://toolradar.com/tools/breeze-chms)
3. **Pushpay ChurchStaq** (large and multi-site churches; unified giving, ChMS and app).
   - 11–14k customers; 7 of the 10 largest US churches.
   - Capterra 4.3 from ~440 reviews.
   - Publishes the industry's main annual tech survey with Barna.
   - Mid-2026 "Staq Transformation" platform rebuild.
   - [Pushpay blog](https://pushpay.com/blog/best-church-management-software-for-2026/); [GlobeNewswire, Jun 30, 2026](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/30/3320253/0/en/Pushpay-Debuts-a-Rebuilt-Groups-Experience-Kicking-Off-Its-Most-Ambitious-Platform-Overhaul-to-Date.html)
4. **Realm by ACS Technologies** (mid to large churches; strong in fund accounting). This matches ChurchTrac's accounting differentiator.
   - Part of the 40k+ church ACS/Vanco combination.
   - Capterra 4.4 from ~273 reviews.
   - ChurchTrac publishes a "Realm alternative" page.
   - On Pushpay's 2026 list.
   - [William Blair](https://www.williamblair.com/News/ACS-Technologies-and-Vanco-Transaction); [ChurchTrac comparisons](https://www.churchtrac.com/comparisons); [Pushpay blog](https://pushpay.com/blog/best-church-management-software-for-2026/)
5. **Rock RMS** (large, tech-capable churches; open source).
   - #1 on ChurchTrac's own 2026 list, which says Rock "powers 10 of the 12 largest churches."
   - Capterra 4.9 from ~101 reviews.
   - Pricing is a voluntary $4.45 per weekly attendee per year.
   - [ChurchTrac blog](https://www.churchtrac.com/blog/best-church-management-software); [Rock RMS FAQ](https://origin.rockrms.com/Spark/FAQ)

**Near misses**
- **Subsplash:** app, media and giving first. 20k+ organizations; Roper paid about $800M in July 2025; Capterra 4.5 from ~136 reviews; on Pushpay's list. Missed because it is app/engagement-first, not ChMS-first. — [Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/news/feed/roper-technologies-acquires-subsplash-for-800m)
- **ChMeetings:** Capterra 4.9 from ~151–167 reviews; appears on several 2026 roundups; low-cost all-in-one. Missed for limited scale evidence and because ChurchTrac's list dismisses it. — [ChurchTrac blog](https://www.churchtrac.com/blog/best-church-management-software)
- **MinistryPlatform (ACS):** large-church platform; only ~49 Capterra reviews. Now under the same owner as Realm. — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/110437/MinistryPlatform/alternatives/)
- **Legacy small-church tools (PowerChurch, Church Windows, Servant Keeper, IconCMO):** frequent ChurchTrac displacement targets, but declining desktop-era products with little 2026 list presence. — [ChurchTrac comparisons](https://www.churchtrac.com/comparisons)
- **Ministry Brands portfolio (ShelbyNext, Elexio, Fellowship One, ParishSOFT):** large roll-up footprint, but little presence in 2026 "best" lists or ChurchTrac comparisons. — [Ministry Brands news](https://ministrybrands.com/news/church-software-icon-fellowship-one-joins-ministry-brands-family)
- **International:** Elvanto (Australia, founded 2010; widely reported as owned by Tithely, but I did not verify the acquisition in this pass) and ChurchSuite (UK, founded 2012; serves UK/Ireland, North America and EU). Not US-focused competitors. — [SourceForge comparison](https://www.sourceforge.net/software/compare/ChurchSuite-vs-Elvanto); [GetApp CA ChurchSuite](https://www.getapp.ca/software/2049793/churchsuite)
- **Accounting-only rivals (Aplos, QuickBooks):** compete with ChurchTrac's accounting module, not its full ChMS. — [ChurchTrac comparisons](https://www.churchtrac.com/comparisons)

### Inferences
- **Suggested segmentation:**
  - Small and mid-church all-in-one (ChurchTrac's home turf): Planning Center, Tithely Church Management, Realm.
  - Large-church and multi-site platforms (set the feature ceiling): Pushpay ChurchStaq, Rock RMS.
- **For a new ministry-leader app,** an alternate set that swaps Rock RMS for Subsplash would better reflect the member-app competition.
- **What the five represent:** about three-quarters of claimed church reach, and every major ownership model:
  - independent / employee-run (Planning Center, ChurchTrac)
  - PE-backed fintech roll-ups (Tithely, Pushpay)
  - PE-backed incumbent (ACS/Vanco)
  - open source (Rock)
  - strategic acquirer (Subsplash/Roper)

### Gaps
- The ranking relies on vendor-claimed counts and review volume. No revenue or seat-share data exists to confirm it.
- Planning Center ownership status in 2024–2026 was not researched.

## 5. Consolidation and ownership: who owns whom, and 2024–2026 deals

### Takeaway
The market has consolidated into a few payments-led groups:
- Tithely (Breeze; Elvanto ownership reported but not verified here)
- Pushpay (CCB, Resi; private since 2023)
- ACS Technologies + Vanco (Realm, MinistryPlatform; closed Dec 2025)
- Subsplash, bought by public strategic buyer Roper for about $800M (Jul 2025)
- Ministry Brands (Fellowship One, Shelby, ParishSOFT and others)
- Gloo as a newly public (Nov 2025) faith-tech consolidator

Planning Center, ChurchTrac and Rock RMS are the notable independents.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- **The economic logic is payments:** owners of giving and payments rails (Tithely, Pushpay, Vanco, Subsplash) buy or bundle ChMS to lock in processing revenue, which explains "free" or cheap ChMS bundled with giving.

## 6. Market trends 2024–2026: AI, mobile, giving + ChMS, pricing, and ministry-leader pain points

### Takeaway
The 2026 survey frames the market as past adoption and now about "alignment." Leaders want ease of use, sensible prices and trusted vendors. Their top three-year priorities are mobile church apps, tap-to-give and multilingual tools. Personal AI use is mainstream, but institutional AI use and governance lag. Giving and ChMS are converging into payments-led bundles, while pricing splits into modular, flat, quote-based and size-tiered models.

## 7. Unmet needs and white space: what ministry leaders need that no product fully offers yet (added Nov 8, 2026)

Method note: "Not offered" means I found no shipping product with the capability in this pass, which used web search, vendor changelogs and idea boards, and review snippets. It is not proof of absence. Search returned no r/churchtech threads, and Capterra and G2 review pages were blocked (403), so complaint evidence is thinner than planned.

### Takeaway
Several capabilities that sound new already exist, at least partly:
- **Predictive disengagement alerts:** Pushpay + Nurture.io, Subsplash Trends AI, startups ChurchAI and Aligned.
- **AI pastoral-care helpers:** Nurture.io; Rock RMS AI prayer moderation and self-harm flags.
- **Discipleship pathway tracking:** Rock Steps, Realm Pathways, Planning Center Workflows.

These are not white space. The strongest evidence of real gaps falls in three areas:
1. **Volunteer burnout and load-risk detection across ministries.** Only rule-based scheduling limits exist.
2. **Consent-based collaboration across independent churches.** Only multi-campus features, network-level aggregate data from Gloo, or small niche tools exist.
3. **A fully multilingual member experience inside the ChMS.** Translation exists only for sermons and content.

Runners-up: AI with church AI-policy controls built in (privacy, redaction, audit), and tracking pastor and staff wellbeing and workload.

### Inferences
- **Recommended three white-space features** (strongest evidence that no product offers them, matched to stated needs):
  1. **Cross-ministry volunteer load and burnout radar.** It would combine serving across all teams, consecutive weeks served, recent declines and no-shows, group and attendance drop-off, and appreciation history. It would warn the right leader and suggest rotation, rather than just capping frequency.
     - Today's partial offerings are rule-based only: Planning Center scheduling preferences and blockout reports, Churchteams tenure/thanks reports, ChMeetings conflict alerts.
  2. **Consent-based cross-church collaboration layer** for independent churches in a city or network: shared volunteer and serve opportunities, benevolence case de-duplication, joint events and registrations, and network dashboards, with member-level consent.
     - Today's partial offerings: Gloo (aggregate network data), Meet the Need (city case management, small and dated), State of Our City (pilot). Mainstream ChMS stop at multi-campus.
  3. **Native multilingual member experience:** each person has a preferred language, and announcements, texts (including WhatsApp), forms, the app and care follow-ups go out auto-translated, with review of the translations.
     - Today's partial offerings: sermon translation tools (OneAccord, Pastors.ai, Gloo); Rock's top idea-board request is WhatsApp texting; no translation work in Planning Center's changelog.
- **Strong alternates:**
  - Policy-aware, privacy-first AI pastoral copilot: enforces the church's AI policy, redacts care and prayer data before it reaches a model, keeps an audit trail. It addresses the 64%-vs-5% policy gap and the 83% privacy concern.
  - Pastor and staff wellbeing and care-load tracking.
- **Avoid "AI churn prediction" as the headline differentiator.** Pushpay/Nurture, Subsplash and several startups shipped it in 2026. A new entrant would have to win on accuracy, explainability, price for small churches, or working across vendors (via the APIs and MCP that Planning Center and Rock are opening up).
- **AI opening up the incumbents is a delivery path:** Planning Center's Aug 2026 MCP server and the Rock "AI MCP Tools" idea suggest a new app could add these white-space features *on top of* incumbent ChMS data rather than replacing the ChMS. That lowers the "working by Sunday" switching barrier.
